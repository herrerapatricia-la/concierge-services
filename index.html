A few helpful details

Before we get started.

How is pricing determined?

Our base service rate is $150 per hour. We confirm the scope, estimated hours and service fee before work begins. Gifts, supplies, shipping and third-party services are separate expenses, agreed upon before purchase.

Can I book help with just one thing?

Yes. Whether it’s one closet, a wardrobe refresh, gift wrapping or a few errands, we can discuss a focused project or a broader plan.

How far in advance should I reach out?

Reach out as soon as you know your needs. Availability and turnaround depend on the scope, your preferred dates and current appointments. Service hours are 8 a.m.–8 p.m.; specific dates are confirmed when booking.

Do you help with business gifting?

Yes. Inquire about gift sourcing, presentation and shipping coordination for clients, colleagues or teams. We’ll confirm quantities, timing and budget together.
