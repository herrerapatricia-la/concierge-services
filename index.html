A few helpful details

Before we get started.

How is pricing determined?

Service fees are quoted based on the scope, timing and level of support you need. Gifts, supplies, shipping and third-party services are separate expenses, agreed upon before purchase.

Can I book help with just one thing?

Yes. Whether it’s a gift list, wrapping or a few holiday errands, we can discuss a focused project or a broader plan.

How far in advance should I reach out?

Reach out as soon as you know your needs. Availability and turnaround depend on the scope, shopping deadlines and the holiday calendar.

Do you help with business gifting?

Yes. Inquire about gift sourcing, presentation and shipping coordination for clients, colleagues or teams. We’ll confirm quantities, timing and budget together.
